01Overview

MyOrganiser is a personal organizer that helps you manage tasks, addresses, contacts, grocery lists, and other personal information, on the web and in the MyOrganiser Mobile App. Your personal content is kept in a Vault that is end-to-end encrypted: it is encrypted on your device with a key only you can unlock, and our servers store only the encrypted version, which we cannot read. Your account data — such as your name and email address — is stored unencrypted, because we need it to operate your account.

02The Mobile App

The MyOrganiser Mobile App for Android is distributed as a download from our download page, not through an app store. On iPhone and iPad there is no app to download: you add the MyOrganiser web app to your Home Screen instead, and this policy covers it as the web app.

The Mobile App uses the same account and the same encrypted Vault as the web app, and this policy applies to it in the same way. With each request it tells our servers its version number and whether it runs on Android or iOS, so we can tell when it needs updating. On your device it keeps your sign-in in the device's secure storage, the key for Biometric Unlock if you turn it on (protected by your fingerprint or face), and your display settings. The Mobile App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

03YouTube Integration

MyOrganiser has an optional YouTube feature. It is a private feature run personally by the operator. It is available only to accounts the operator has enabled in MyOrganiser; without that, YouTube is not available to your account. It is not offered to the public.

The feature uses YouTube API Services. If your account has it and you connect your YouTube account through Google OAuth, MyOrganiser requests the youtube.readonly scope, which gives MyOrganiser read-only access to your YouTube subscriptions and the uploads of those channels — with no ability to write, delete, or modify anything on YouTube. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy ↗.

What we store: For the channels you follow we store the channel ID, title, and thumbnail. For their recent uploads we store the video ID, title, thumbnail, publish date, and duration, and which uploads you have marked as watched. We never store the video files themselves. This metadata is kept on our servers and is not part of the end-to-end encrypted Vault. Your YouTube OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest on our servers with AES-256-GCM.

How we use and share it: We use this data only to show you new uploads from your channels inside MyOrganiser and, if you opt in, to email you a digest of them. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. It is stored with our hosting provider, and a digest you opted in to is sent through our email delivery provider. It is not shared with anyone else, and no other MyOrganiser user can see it. MyOrganiser's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy ↗, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion: You can end this connection at any time by disconnecting YouTube from the YouTube page in your dashboard, or by revoking MyOrganiser's access from your Google security settings ↗. Disconnecting revokes MyOrganiser's access at Google and immediately deletes your stored tokens, followed channels, cached uploads, watched marks, and notification and digest settings. If you revoke MyOrganiser's access at Google instead, MyOrganiser stops syncing and deletes the same data within seven days unless you reconnect. The operator may also withdraw the feature from an account; doing so deletes the same data as a disconnect, revoking MyOrganiser's access at Google and deleting your stored tokens, followed channels, cached uploads, watched marks, and notification and digest settings.

For detailed information about how long we keep your YouTube metadata and how to request deletion, see our YouTube Data Privacy page.

04Account Data

We collect and store the information you provide when creating and managing your account: your name, your email address, a hash of your password (never the password itself), and a User ID that identifies your account. We use this data only to sign you in and operate your account — not for advertising or tracking — and we do not sell or share it.

05Cookies & Sessions

MyOrganiser uses session cookies to keep you signed in. These cookies are secure, encrypted, and removed when you sign out or they expire. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

06Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time: in the Mobile App under Account, and on the web on your Account page. Before deleting, you are offered an Escape Copy — a download of your encrypted Vault that you keep. You then confirm with your password and by typing DELETE.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your account, your encrypted Vault, and all your YouTube data from our servers, ends your YouTube connection, and signs you out everywhere. We send a confirmation to your email address. Our security logs keep a record that administrative actions took place, with your account removed from them. The Mobile App also removes your sign-in and Biometric Unlock key from the device. An Escape Copy you downloaded, or an encrypted copy of your Vault kept in your web browser, stays on your own devices and is yours to delete.

07Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, and delete your account and associated data at any time. You can export your Vault data from the Vault page on the web, and delete your account as described above. To request a complete copy of your data, or for help deleting it, contact us using the information below.
