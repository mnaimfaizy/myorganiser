How we store your YouTube data
  • 1We store metadata only — titles, thumbnails, video IDs, publish times, duration, and channel info. We never store the video files themselves.
  • 2Watched is a simple yes/no marker for what you've already seen. It is not a viewing history or analytics feature — no progress percentages, watch counts, or archives.
  • 3We keep the latest 100 Cached Uploads per Enabled Channel.
  • 4If you disable a channel, its Cached Uploads and Watched status are kept for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • 5Disconnecting your YouTube account immediately deletes Followed Channels, Cached Uploads, Watched marks, notification and digest settings, and OAuth tokens. Nothing is kept for a later reconnect.
  • 6Your Shorts Daily Budget is tracked locally in your browser, not on our servers.
  • 7This YouTube metadata is not covered by MyOrganiser's Vault or end-to-end encryption — those apply elsewhere in the app. (Your YouTube sign-in tokens are still encrypted at rest.)
