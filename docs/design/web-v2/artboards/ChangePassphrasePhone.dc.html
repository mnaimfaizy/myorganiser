Change passphrase

Choose a new passphrase for unlocking your vault on this device.

Your data is not re-encrypted and nothing is decrypted on the server — only what unlocks your vault changes. Your recovery key still works and does not need to be written down again. Your other devices keep using the old passphrase until you confirm the change on each of them; they will ask the next time they sync.
Minimum 10 characters.
